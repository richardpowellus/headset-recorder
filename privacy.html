Headset Recorder privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary

Headset Recorder records audio on your PC and transcribes it on your PC. It does not send your audio, transcripts, or any other personal information anywhere. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app accesses

  • Your microphone, to record what you say during a recording you start.
  • Audio playing to the output device you choose (for example your headset), to record what you hear during a recording you start.
  • A folder you choose, where recordings and transcripts are saved.

Recording happens only between the moment you choose Start recording and the moment you stop it. While recording, the notification-area icon is red.

What the app stores

All of it on your PC, in the folder you choose:

  • The recording, in the format you pick in Settings (Opus, AAC, MP3, FLAC or WAV).
  • Two hidden audio tracks (.remote.opus, .me.opus): the other side and your microphone kept apart, so the transcript can tell who said what. They stay next to the recording.
  • Transcripts (.md, .html, .json) and the live transcript (.jsonl), including the names the app learned from the conversation or that you typed in. Small hidden working files (.live.json, .pending) are deleted when the transcript is finished.

You can move or delete any of these at any time; uninstalling the app does not delete them.

In the app's private storage (removed when you uninstall): your settings, and a diagnostic log (app.log) of what the app did: device names, file names, timings and errors, never audio or transcript text. Settings > About > Open log folder shows it.

The speech-recognition and speaker models are part of the app package. The app downloads nothing.

What the app sends

Nothing. Transcription, speaker labelling and name detection run entirely on your PC's processor and graphics card, and the app makes no network connections. The app sends no telemetry. If the app crashes, Windows may send a crash report to Microsoft according to your Windows diagnostic-data settings; the publisher sees only aggregated crash data in Microsoft Partner Center.

Recording other people

Laws in many places require the consent of everyone on a call before it is recorded. The app asks you to acknowledge this before first use and can remind you to tell others when a recording starts. You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
